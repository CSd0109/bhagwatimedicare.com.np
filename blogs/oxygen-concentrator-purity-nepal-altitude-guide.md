## 🏔️ Why Altitude Impacts Oxygen Output in Nepal

At sea level, atmospheric pressure is 101.3 kPa (760 mmHg). In Kathmandu Valley (1,400 meters) and hilly/mountainous terrain across Nepal (Pokhara, Mustang, Solukhumbu, Manang), atmospheric pressure drops significantly under 80 kPa.

Non-medical or cheap portable concentrators suffer steep purity drop-offs at higher altitudes. A clinical-grade oxygen concentrator in Nepal must have:
1. **Dual-Cylinder High-Efficiency Molecular Sieve:** French & German imported lithium or synthetic zeolite beds capable of high nitrogen adsorption under thin air.
2. **Integrated Oxygen Purity Sensor (OCSI):** Ultrasonic real-time monitoring ensuring delivery never drops below 90% purity.
3. **Heavy-Duty Oil-Free Compressor:** Engineered for 24/7 continuous non-stop operation during power cuts and voltage fluctuations.
