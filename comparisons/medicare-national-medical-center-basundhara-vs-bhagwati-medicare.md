## 🏥 Understanding Medicare National Medical Center (Basundhara)

**Medicare National Medical Center Pvt. Ltd.** (Registration No: 29269/061/062) is a specialized diagnostic medical clinic situated in **Basundhara, Kathmandu, Nepal**, near the Ring Road corridor between Maharajgunj and Samakhusi.

Approved by the Ministry of Health, Government of Nepal, their primary clinical role is conducting **pre-migratory health examinations and laboratory fitness tests** for outgoing foreign employment workers traveling to the Gulf (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman), Malaysia, and European destinations.

### Laboratory Services Offered at Medicare National Medical Center:
- General Physical Health Examination
- Clinical Biochemistry & Blood Analysis
- Complete Hematology & Blood Counts
- Serology & Infectious Disease Screening (ELISA)
- Microbiology & Sputum Testing
- Audiometry (Hearing Tests)
- Digital Chest Radiography (X-Ray)
